Effective Communication

Presentation Delivery Skills

  • Stand balanced with an open, natural posture; avoid crossing arms or slouching.

  • Maintain regular eye contact across the room to connect with the entire audience.

  • Speak clearly with suitable volume and pace so everyone can hear and understand.

  • Vary your vocal tone and pitch to emphasize key points and maintain interest.

  • Use purposeful hand gestures to support your message, but avoid fidgeting.

  • Use strategic pauses to highlight important ideas and check for understanding.

The 3 Vs of Communication

"Verbal (Words), Vocal (Tone), and Visual (Body Language) must all align."
