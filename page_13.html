Presentation Delivery

Don'ts During Presentation

  • Do not read directly from the slides; this breaks connection with the audience.

  • Avoid crowding slides with excessive text or complex paragraphs.

  • Do not speak too fast or too softly; ensure your voice reaches the back of the room.

  • Eliminate filler words like "um," "uh," and "you know" to sound more professional.

  • Never turn your back completely to the audience to look at the screen.

  • Avoid arguing with the audience; handle difficult questions or challenges calmly.

Critical Warning

"Your slides are there to support you, not to replace you."
