Handling Nervousness

  • Practice aloud multiple times using a timer to simulate the actual environment.

  • Utilize deep breathing exercises (inhale deep, exhale slow) to physically calm your body.

  • Replace negative self-talk with positive affirmations to shift your mindset.

  • Thoroughly rehearse your opening and closing lines until they feel effortless.

  • Keep water handy and maintain a grounded posture to reduce physical tension.

  • Prepare a backup plan for technology failures to eliminate anxiety about the unknown.

Confidence Tip

"Nervousness is just excitement without breath. Breathe through it and it becomes energy."
