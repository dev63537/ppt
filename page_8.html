Designing Effective Slides

  • Keep slides simple and uncluttered to maintain audience focus on the message.

  • Limit content to short bullet points (4–6 per slide) rather than full sentences.

  • Ensure text is readable from the back by using a minimum font size of 24–28 pt.

  • Maintain high contrast between text and background (e.g., dark text on light background).

  • Use images, diagrams, or charts only when they aid understanding, not just for decoration.

  • Establish a consistent layout and color scheme throughout the entire presentation.

Visual Rule

"Less is often more. White space is not empty space; it is an active design element."
