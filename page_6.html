Planning the Presentation

  • Organize your content into three distinct parts: Introduction, Body, and Conclusion.

  • Ensure a logical sequence of ideas (flow) to maintain audience engagement throughout.

  • Utilize signposting language (e.g., "First," "Next," "Finally") to guide the audience.

  • Incorporate smooth transitions to connect different sections and concepts seamlessly.

  • Allocate specific time slots for each section to adhere strictly to the time limit.

  • Design a clear, memorable closing message that reinforces the presentation's core objective.

The "Rule of Three"

"Tell them what you are going to tell them, tell them, then tell them what you told them."
