Content Writing Tips

  • Use keywords and short phrases rather than writing out full paragraphs.

  • Focus on just one main idea per slide to maintain clarity and focus.

  • Avoid information overload; too much text forces the audience to read instead of listen.

  • Define any technical terms or jargon that the audience may not be familiar with.

  • Support your key points with brief, relevant examples or data points.

  • Always cite your sources for facts and images to ensure academic integrity.

The "Less is More" Rule

"Your slides are there to support you, not to replace you. Keep text minimal."
