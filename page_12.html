Presentation Delivery

Do's During Presentation

  • Speak clearly and at a steady pace to ensure everyone understands your message.

  • Engage the audience effectively by asking thought-provoking questions or using relevant examples.

  • Face the audience confidently; use the slides only as a support tool, not a script.

  • Monitor your time carefully and adjust your pacing if you are running ahead or behind schedule.

  • Summarize key points at transition moments to reinforce learning and guide the narrative flow.

  • Acknowledge questions politely and provide brief, focused answers to maintain momentum.
